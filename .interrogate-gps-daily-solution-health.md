# Destination: Daily GNSS solution availability in Grafana

## Problem
Operators need to know whether expected daily processed GNSS solutions are available, and see upstream receiver/data-delivery context when they are not. Existing receivers “24h” delivery indicators do not establish processed-solution completeness. “24 solutions” means a solution from a 24-hour session, not 24 hourly results.

## In scope
- One logical daily POS stream; currently `imo.24hr_itrf14` under `/mnt/data/gpsops/pos` on yang. Producer may move to a GAMIT processing server.
- Configurable read-only solution directory, preferably NFS; isolated temporary SSH access permitted. Checker initially on rek-d01; no permanent producer-hostname dependency.
- Explicit IMO daily-processing roster with expectation dates; never infer expectations from existing files or active receivers alone.
- Green certifies a correct station/day record with finite, readable coordinates at the source—not downstream consumer ingestion or scientific quality.
- Publisher emits an atomic batch-completion record; check promptly after completion, with periodic fallback. Normal arrival 08:30–09:00 UTC; unfinished delivery becomes late after 09:15 UTC.
- Recheck a rolling 45-day window, detecting revisions and removals as well as arrivals.
- Persist solution observations and check freshness separately for Grafana. Main view: station × UTC-day grid, exception summary, station links and upstream receiver/raw/RINEX context.
- Distinguish available, pending, overdue, invalid, not expected and unknown. Unfinished publication is not confirmed missing data; failed/stale access means unknown.

## Out of scope
Scientific quality thresholds, downstream-ingestion certification, multiple streams initially, notifications, automatic recovery, reprocessing and repairs.

## Definition of done
Expected station-days are inspectable; historical gaps are not hidden by newer results. Failed/stale checks cannot imply completeness or confirmed absence. Real POS samples and controlled missing/unreadable/check-failure cases demonstrate the agreed states.

## Hard constraints
Design approved; next authorization is planning only. No implementation, deployment or production writes. Verify shared-path/service-account access, authoritative roster and exact publication/validation contracts before implementation; never silently substitute products.

## Downstream
Prepare a reviewable implementation plan with independently mergeable vertical slices and validation gates.
